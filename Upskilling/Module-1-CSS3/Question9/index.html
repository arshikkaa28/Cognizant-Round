Community Bulletin

The city council is organizing several community events this month to encourage public participation. Residents can join tree plantation drives, blood donation camps, cleanliness campaigns, and health awareness programs. These activities help create a cleaner, healthier, and greener environment for everyone. Volunteers are requested to register online before attending the events. Free refreshments, participation certificates, and community service recognition will be provided to all participants. Citizens of all age groups are welcome to contribute and make the events successful. Together, we can build a stronger and more connected community for the future.
